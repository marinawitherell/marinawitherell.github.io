Hello, my name is **Marina Witherell**.
\
\
I grew up in Anchorage, Alaska, where I explored my love for the outdoors as well as my love for learning new skills. 
I still consider Alaska to be my home, but am ready to explore new opportunities and start the next chapter of my life.
\
\
I attended Oregon State University, with a major in **Biological Data Sciences** and triple minors in **Computer Science**, 
**Mathematics**, and **Statistics**. The major itself was interdiciplinary, with classes in biology, computer science, 
mathematics, and statistics. I found that I enjoyed the computational side of data science the most, so I decided to 
persue a more in-depth understanding of each of those aspects (hence the minors). I am always of the opinion that if you 
are going to do something, you should do it right the first time. So, I tend to research more than needed for my tasks, 
but I enjoy learing so much that it is not an issue for me. I love feeling confident in my work and knowing that I put 
my best efforts in to produce a final product. You can see more about my professional background on the Resume page.
\
\
In the future, I hope to become a **Data Scientist**, or have some career in that realm. I love working with large datasets 
that represent some aspect of humanity. I think that one of the most compelling things about data is what it can say 
about the people that it concerns and what predictions we can make from that.
\
\
Beyond my professional life, my hobbies include website design, crocheting, knitting, hiking, figure skating, and playing 
the cello. I also love problem solving and am an avid sudoku player!
